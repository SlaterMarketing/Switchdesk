Privacy

We don't collect anything about you.

Switchdesk has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. It doesn't send your code or your chats to us, because there's no "us" server to send them to. This website sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

The app

Switchdesk runs on your computer. Everything it connects to is listed here, and almost all of it only happens when you choose it. Anything you type into an agent goes to that agent's maker, not to us.

WhatWho it talks toWhen
Your agentsThe agent's own maker (Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub, Cursor, Moonshot, Google, Alibaba or DeepSeek)When you chat. Each runs the maker's own CLI with your sign-in or API key, under that maker's terms and privacy policy, exactly as it would in a terminal.
UpdatesGitHubAt start-up and every four hours, to check this project's releases.
Tab autocompleteGitHub, or DeepSeek or AlibabaWhen you've signed in to Copilot or added a DeepSeek or Qwen key. Code near your cursor goes to that engine with your own account or key. Settings → Autocomplete turns it off.
GitHub and LinearGitHub, LinearOnly after you connect them, with your own token.
Connected servicesThe service you ask for (Jira, Notion, Sentry and so on)Only after you sign in to it. The sign-in happens in your browser, and the token stays on your computer.
Search by meaningHugging Face and jsDelivrOff unless you turn it on. It downloads a small model once, then runs on your computer.
Phone check-inYour own phone, over your Wi-Fi or TailscaleOff unless you turn it on. Nothing passes through the internet or through us, and a phone has to scan the QR code before it can see anything.
Report a problemGitHubOpens a new GitHub issue in your browser with your version and system filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it there, and issues are public.

API keys and sign-in tokens you give Switchdesk are encrypted with your operating system's keychain and stored only on your computer. Agents' own logins stay with each agent's CLI, and Switchdesk never sees them.

This website

switchdesk.dev is a static page with no cookies, no analytics and no forms. Its fonts are served from this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS.

Download buttons and star count

Your browser asks GitHub's API for the latest release and the star count, so the buttons can point at the right installer for your computer.

Product Hunt badge

From 31 October 2026, the home page shows a badge image loaded from Product Hunt.

Links out

The video tour is on YouTube and the code is on GitHub. Those sites' own policies apply once you follow a link.

Your data and your rights

We don't hold personal data about people who use Switchdesk or visit this site, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. Under UK GDPR you can still ask us what we hold, and we'll answer. For data held by an agent's maker or another service, ask that company directly.

If you open an issue or pull request on GitHub, it's public on GitHub under your account, and GitHub's privacy statement applies.

Using the agents

Switchdesk is free software under the MIT licence, which is all the terms the app has. Each agent is used under its maker's own terms, so sign in with your own plan or API key, and don't share one plan between several people.

Changes

If this changes, the new version goes here with a new date. Every past version is in the site's history on GitHub.
